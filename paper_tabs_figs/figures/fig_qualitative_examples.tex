Four CIFAR-10 members flagged by LiRA-256 at FPR$=10^{-4}$, with their

the reference fits it well (LT-BTM-R outside the top-10\%).
(c)~The target trajectory is stable, but the reference's loss stays high
even in its best epochs (LT-IQR outside the top-10\%).
(d)~Neither component alone reaches its top-$3\%$, yet their sum
places the sample in \rare{}'s top-$3\%$: two moderate signals
add up to high vulnerability.
